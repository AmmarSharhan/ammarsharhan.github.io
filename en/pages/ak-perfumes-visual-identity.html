How Does an Idea Become a Marketable Visual Identity?

📅 2026 ⏱️ 5 min read ✍️ Ammar Sharhan

A visual identity is not a logo placed on a product and then forgotten. For a perfume brand, the same story should be felt in the bottle, photography, advertising, and presentation.

The process starts with a simple question: what feeling should customers associate with the product? Then come the visual decisions—colors, lighting, backgrounds, wardrobe, composition, and advertising tone.

If every advertisement looks unrelated to the previous one, an individual post may perform, but the brand becomes harder to remember.

The images can vary by scene while staying consistent through:

  • A defined color palette.
  • A recognizable lighting style.
  • A consistent way of presenting the product without changing its identity.
  • A short message connecting the product to a story or feeling.

This turns visual identity from a design file into a system that helps a brand produce consistent content across campaigns and platforms.
