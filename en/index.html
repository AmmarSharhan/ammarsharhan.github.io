About Me

I combine marketing experience with content creation and AI to turn ideas into actionable content and marketing solutions.

I’m Ammar Sharhan, a digital marketing specialist and AI content creator. I have worked in marketing and content since 2013, combining marketing planning, content creation, social media management, and the practical use of AI tools.

I also turn ideas into executable content, from research and concept development to scripting, production, publishing, performance analysis, and content optimization.
